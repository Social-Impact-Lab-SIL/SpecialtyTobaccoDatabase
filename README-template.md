# Patterns of Specialty Tobacco Retail Locations and Visitor Counts in the United States

**Status:** Under Review  
**Maintainer:** Austin Landini, Economic Research Consultant, University of Missouri Social Impacts Lab  
**Last Updated:** March 2026  

## Overview  

We develop a national database of specialty tobacco retailers in the United States by combining Google Maps business listings with Advan unique visitor count data.  

## Repository Structure

├── manuscript/     # Submitted Manuscript  
├── README-template.md  
└── README-replication.md  


## Requirements  

Stata  

## How to Run  

Using proprietary Advan data in combination with data from Google maps, download DraftCodeFinal.do into the same folder as the data files and run.   

## Data Sources  

Raw visitor count data from Advan- Subscription service via DeweyData.io  
Store Counts from Google- as of May 2024  
US County and Census Tract Shapefiles- from Census TigerLines  
Covariates drawn from 2019 ACS US Census Tract 5-Year Estimates.
